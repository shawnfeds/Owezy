# ADR-010: Shared Item Splitting Model

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Bills often contain items shared among a subset of participants (e.g. an appetizer shared by 3 out of 5 people). We need a clear, simple splitting rule for v1.

## Decision
1. **Equal Split Model**: When an item is claimed by $N$ participants, the cost of that item is divided equally among those $N$ participants.
   $$\text{Participant Item Share} = \frac{\text{Line Total}}{N}$$
2. **No Per-Person Quantity Tracking**: v1 will NOT attempt to track individual consumption quantities (e.g. Alice ate 2 slices of a 6-slice pizza). Quantity is an item-level attribute (`Quantity * UnitPrice = LineTotal`).
3. **Combination**: A participant's total unrounded share is the sum of their equal shares across all items they claimed, plus their equal portion of any bill-level fees/discounts/taxes.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Simple domain model, clear UI for item claiming, zero friction.
- **Negative**: Does not support weighted/custom consumption splits in v1.
- **Mitigation**: Aligns 100% with v1 scope control decision.
