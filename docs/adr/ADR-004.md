# ADR-004: Participant Access Model

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Participants must claim items, view their individual share, initiate UPI payments, and mark their split as paid without facing friction such as mandatory user registration, app downloads, or passwords.

## Decision
1. **Relationship-Scoped Access Pattern**: Participants receive a unique, participant-specific link formatted as:
   ```text
   /split/{billToken}/{participantToken}
   ```
   The participant token is NOT a globally meaningful participant identity. Access is strictly scoped to the specific `(Bill, Participant)` relationship.
2. **Access Category**: **"Scoped read access + limited payment-status mutation"**.
   - Read: Own participant info, claimed items, calculated share total, payment status, and required splitter payment collection info (UPI VPA).
   - Limited Mutation: Mark own payment status as paid (`Unpaid` -> `MarkedPaid`).
   - Prohibited: Modifying bill, modifying other participants, viewing other participants' financial info, changing other participants' payment states, finalizing bills, managing bills, accessing splitter history.
3. **Token Security**:
   - `billToken` and `participantToken` MUST be cryptographically random (e.g. 256-bit URL-safe Base64 strings).
   - Tokens MUST NOT be derivable from sequentially incrementing IDs, participant names, bill IDs, or phone numbers.
   - Tokens grant privileges strictly limited to that specific participant's share on that specific bill.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Zero onboarding friction for bill participants, high viral usability, strict data scoping.
- **Negative**: If a participant forwards their link, the recipient can view their split.
- **Mitigation**: Individual participant tokens can be revoked or re-issued by the authenticated Splitter if compromised.
